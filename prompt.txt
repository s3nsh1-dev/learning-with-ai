A natural next topic is **how your Docker app becomes a website people can access**.

You now understand how the app runs and how its parts connect. Next, follow a request from someone’s browser to your API:

1. **Server and deployment** — where your containers run when your laptop is off.
2. **Domain names and DNS** — how `myapp.com` points to that server.
3. **Reverse proxy** — how one entry point receives requests and sends them to the right container.
4. **HTTPS** — how the connection between the browser and server is encrypted.

I’d start with **“What happens when someone opens my deployed app’s URL?”** That gives you the overall picture before learning individual tools such as Nginx or Caddy.

After that, CI/CD makes more sense: you’ll understand what your automated deployment is actually doing.
